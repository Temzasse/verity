# Verity

Verity helps readers examine manipulative rhetoric and factual claims in articles.

## Language

**Claim**:
A single factual assertion that can be assessed independently against evidence; one sentence may contain several claims. Identifying a claim as worth checking does not establish that it is false.

**Claim to check**:
A claim identified for factual verification whose truth has not yet been established through relevant external evidence. This initial label makes no judgment that the claim is false.
_Avoid_: Misinformation, false claim before verification

**External evidence**:
Material outside the article used to assess a claim, such as published fact-checks, official records, or original research. It must address the same assertion and relevant circumstances to support a factual verdict.
_Avoid_: Model confidence, AI inference

**Published claim review**:
A publisher's existing assessment of a specific factual claim, with its own rating and supporting explanation. It can inform Verity's assessment when it addresses the same assertion and circumstances.

**Factual verdict**:
Verity's assessment of a claim based on relevant external evidence. It is distinct from a publisher's original rating and from a finding about rhetorical technique.

**Mixed evidence**:
A factual verdict for unresolved disagreement between credible sources addressing the same claim and circumstances. Differences explained by dates, definitions, or scope do not by themselves constitute mixed evidence.
